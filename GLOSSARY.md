# Setubandh AI

A multi-tenant shared inbox where businesses answer customers on WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger, with an AI assistant that can reply for them. "Orbit" in `docs/` is the working name for the same product.

## Language

### Tenancy

**Workspace**:
One customer business using the product; the unit of isolation, billing and limits. A user can belong to several workspaces.
_Avoid_: Account, organisation, company (in code, "tenant" is acceptable as a synonym)

**Platform**:
The product itself as run by its operator, and the data that sits above any single workspace (plans, workspaces, invoices).
_Avoid_: System, global

**Membership**:
A user's place in one workspace, with one role.
_Avoid_: Seat, team member record

### Channels and people

**Channel**:
One connected messaging account of a workspace: a WhatsApp number, a Facebook Page (Messenger and comments) or an Instagram professional account (DMs and comments).
_Avoid_: Integration, connection, inbox

**Contact**:
A customer of a workspace. One contact can have several channel identities.
_Avoid_: Customer record, lead (a lead is a sales opportunity, not a person), user

**Agent**:
A workspace member who replies to contacts from the inbox.
_Avoid_: Operator, rep, user (when meaning a member who answers)

**WABA**:
A WhatsApp Business Account, owned by the workspace inside its Meta Business portfolio.

### Conversations

**Conversation**:
A thread between one contact and the workspace on one channel, with a status, a mode and an assignee.
_Avoid_: Chat, ticket, thread

**Mode**:
Who drives a conversation: `ai` (the AI replies), `human` (the AI is silent) or `assist` (the AI drafts, a human sends).
_Avoid_: State, status (status is open or resolved)

**Takeover**:
An agent moving a conversation from `ai` to `human` mode.
_Avoid_: Override, intercept

**Handoff**:
The AI moving a conversation to `human` mode, with a reason and a summary for the agent.
_Avoid_: Escalation, transfer

**Hand back**:
An agent returning a conversation from `human` to `ai` mode.

**Service window**:
The 24-hour period, opened or reset by each customer message, in which free-form replies are allowed. Outside it, WhatsApp only allows templates.
_Avoid_: CSW, session window, 24h rule

**Template**:
A WhatsApp message pre-approved by Meta in a category (marketing, utility, authentication); the only thing that can be sent outside the service window.
_Avoid_: Canned message (that is a saved reply), HSM

**Saved reply**:
A reusable message an agent can insert into the composer.
_Avoid_: Canned response, quick reply (quick replies are channel buttons)

**Private reply**:
A DM sent to a person in answer to their public comment on a Facebook or Instagram post.

### Sales and AI

**Lead**:
A sales opportunity linked to a contact, sitting in a pipeline stage.
_Avoid_: Deal, prospect, opportunity

**Knowledge base**:
The products, FAQs and documents of a workspace that the AI answers from.
_Avoid_: KB (in prose), catalogue (the catalogue is only the products)

**AI reply**:
One outbound message generated by the AI; the unit counted against plan quotas.
_Avoid_: AI message, credit

**Tool**:
A function the AI can call to read facts or take actions, such as searching products or handing off.
_Avoid_: Function, action, plugin

### Meta platform

**Tech Provider**:
Meta's programme that lets this product onboard other businesses' WhatsApp accounts through Embedded Signup under one platform Meta app.
